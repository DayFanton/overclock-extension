# Privacy Policy for Overclock Chrome Extension

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Day_Fanton ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information that we receive when you use our Chrome extension, Overclock (the "Extension").

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
The Extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or any other data that can be used to identify you.

### B. Usage Data and Content
We do not track your browsing history, the websites you visit, or the content you interact with while using the Extension. All media control functions (speed adjustment, seeking, timecodes) are performed locally within your browser.

### C. Local Storage
The Extension utilizes the `chrome.storage` API solely to store your personalized settings, such as custom hotkeys, overlay appearance preferences, and the website blacklist. This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

## 2. No Internet Permissions Required for Operation
The Extension does not require or request permission to access the internet to communicate with remote servers. All operations are conducted locally on your machine.

## 3. Support for the Developer
The Extension is free to use. Users may voluntarily choose to support the developer through donation links provided in the Extension's settings. This process is voluntary and does not involve the collection of payment details by the Extension itself.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support.overclock@gmail.com.
